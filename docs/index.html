MarginIQ

A flat white sells for the same price everywhere. What the café keeps does not. Load your sales and MarginIQ shows which items lose money on which delivery app, and what to do about each one.

Your data

Paste sales instead, or see the format

One row per sale, with a header row. Columns can be in any order and are matched by name: item, channel, quantity (or qty), and a date or timestamp (sold_at, date, time). Dates can be 2026-09-14 or 14/09/2026.

Your menu and fees

These drive every figure below. Replace the sample numbers with yours. Packaging is charged on delivery orders only. A channel with 0% commission is treated as dine-in or pickup.

MarginIQ calculates profit per sale as price times quantity, less commission, ingredient cost and (for delivery) packaging. It then subtracts an overhead assumption and applies a rule to each item and channel. The same logic runs as a PostgreSQL function in the project's SQL model. The sample café is synthetic data built to mimic a Melbourne café, so its results show how the method works, not a real business.
